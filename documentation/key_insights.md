# Key Insights

## Overview

The Power BI dashboard was developed to identify key patterns in revenue, profitability, product performance, customer behavior, and store performance.

The following insights were derived from the completed analysis.

---

## 1. Overall Business Performance

- Total revenue reached approximately **$55.76 million**.
- Total profit was approximately **$32.66 million**.
- Overall profit margin was approximately **58.58%**.
- A total of approximately **26,326 unique orders** were recorded.
- The analysis covered approximately **11,887 unique customers**.

---

## 2. Revenue Trend

Revenue increased significantly between 2016 and 2019.

- **2019** was the strongest year, generating approximately **$18.26 million** in revenue.
- Revenue declined to approximately **$9.29 million** in 2020.
- The reported revenue for 2021 was approximately **$1.04 million**.

The yearly trend indicates substantial variation in sales performance across the analysis period.

---

## 3. Category Performance

**Computers** was the highest revenue-generating category, contributing approximately **$19.30 million** in revenue.

Other major categories included:

- Home Appliances — approximately **$10.80M**
- Cameras and Camcorders — approximately **$6.52M**
- Cell Phones — approximately **$6.18M**
- TV and Video — approximately **$5.93M**

Computers also generated the highest total profit among the major categories.

---

## 4. Product Performance

Desktop PC products appeared prominently among the highest-performing products.

The highest-revenue product was:

**WWI Desktop PC2.33 X2330 Black**

with approximately **$505K** in revenue.

Several other desktop PC variants also appeared among the highest-revenue products, indicating strong demand for computer products.

---

## 5. Brand Performance

**Adventure Works** generated the highest total revenue among the analyzed brands at approximately **$11.85 million**.

Other major contributors included:

- Contoso — approximately **$10.79M**
- Wide World Importers — approximately **$9.17M**
- Fabrikam — approximately **$6.81M**

This shows that revenue was concentrated among a relatively small number of leading brands.

---

## 6. Geographic Performance

The **United States** was the largest revenue-generating country, contributing approximately **$29.87 million**.

Other major markets included:

- United Kingdom — approximately **$7.08M**
- Germany — approximately **$5.41M**
- Canada — approximately **$4.72M**

North America also had the largest customer base, followed by Europe and Australia.

---

## 7. Store Performance

Physical stores generated approximately **79.55%** of total revenue, while online sales contributed approximately **20.45%**.

This indicates that physical retail remained the dominant sales channel in the analyzed dataset.

The Online Store was also the highest-revenue individual store.

---

## 8. Customer Insights

The analysis recorded approximately **11,887 unique customers**.

Average revenue per customer was approximately **$4,690.46**, while the average number of orders per customer was approximately **2.21**.

This indicates that customers placed multiple orders on average rather than making only a single purchase.

---

## 9. Average Order Value

Average Order Value showed a declining trend from 2016 through 2019:

- 2016 — approximately **$2,503**
- 2017 — approximately **$2,228**
- 2018 — approximately **$2,163**
- 2019 — approximately **$1,965**

AOV then recovered slightly:

- 2020 — approximately **$2,022**
- 2021 — approximately **$2,145**

Despite the lower AOV in 2019, overall revenue reached its highest level that year, indicating that higher order volume contributed significantly to total revenue.

---

## 10. Business Takeaways

The analysis highlights several areas that could be useful for business decision-making:

- **Computers** are a major driver of both revenue and profitability.
- The **United States** represents the strongest individual geographic market.
- **Physical stores** remain the dominant revenue channel.
- A relatively small group of **brands and products contributes significantly to total revenue**.
- Revenue growth should be evaluated together with **order volume and Average Order Value**, rather than relying on revenue alone.
- Customer and geographic analysis can help identify opportunities for improving customer retention and channel performance.

---

## Conclusion

The Power BI analysis converts the underlying retail data into an interactive business intelligence report covering three major perspectives:

1. **Executive Overview**
2. **Product Performance Analysis**
3. **Customer & Store Insights**

Together, these dashboards provide a consolidated view of the retailer's sales performance and demonstrate the use of Power BI for data transformation, modeling, DAX-based KPI development, and business analysis.
